About me

I'm a full stack developer and IT manager from Tarlac, Philippines. Most of my work is internal business software — the purchasing, inventory, warehouse and reporting systems a company depends on every day. I write them, deploy them, and then keep them running.

My core stack is Laravel on the backend with Livewire or Inertia + React + TypeScript on the front, backed by PostgreSQL or MySQL, Redis queues and WebSockets for anything that needs to feel live. On the Node side I work with Express, Vite and Supabase.

Two things shape most of my recent work. First, AI integration — using the Claude, OpenAI and Gemini APIs for structured document extraction, classification and retrieval, so a PDF invoice becomes a validated record instead of a typing job. Second, automation — approval routing, queued jobs, scheduled reports, webhooks and n8n flows that remove the repetitive steps between systems.

I also handle the operations side: Ubuntu servers, Docker Compose, Nginx reverse proxies with automated Let's Encrypt certificates, and deployments across GCP, AWS and DigitalOcean. Being on both sides of the wall means I design for what actually survives production.
